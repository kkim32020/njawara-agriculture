About Us

The Njawara Agriculture Training Center (NATC) is a community-led organization founded in 1990 and registered in 1997. It is currently led by Director Al Pha Sey.

Mission

To train farmers in sustainable agriculture and improve livelihoods in rural communities.

Location

Njawara Village, North Bank Region, The Gambia
