About Us

The Njawara Agriculture Training Center (NATC) is a community-led organization founded in 1990 and registered as an NGO in 1997. The center is currently led by Director Al Pha Sey, who continues to guide its mission of strengthening rural livelihoods through sustainable agriculture.

Located in Njawara Village, the center includes training facilities and demonstration fields where both men and women learn improved farming techniques designed to increase productivity while protecting the environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to work in partnership with local and international institutions to train farmers in sustainable agricultural systems. We aim to promote self-reliance, increase employment opportunities, reduce poverty, and discourage rural-to-urban migration.

Our Vision

We envision stronger rural communities with improved livelihoods, reliable food systems, and increased economic opportunities.

What We Do

  • Crop production and demonstration plots
  • Agroforestry and integration of trees with farmland
  • Soil and water conservation techniques
  • Livestock and crop integration
  • Pest management and climate resilience strategies
