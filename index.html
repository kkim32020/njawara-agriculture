About Us

The Njawara Agriculture Training Center (NATC) is a community-led organization founded in 1990 by Mr. Badarra Jobe, a native of Njawara Village and the local Alkalo. It became a registered NGO in 1997. NATC was established to support rural farmers and youth through practical, hands-on training in sustainable and ecological agriculture.

Located in Njawara Village, the center includes training facilities and demonstration fields where both men and women learn improved farming techniques designed to increase productivity while protecting the environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to work in partnership with local and international institutions to train farmers in sustainable agricultural systems. We aim to promote self-reliance, increase employment opportunities, reduce poverty, and discourage rural-to-urban migration.

Our Vision

We envision stronger rural communities with improved livelihoods, reliable food systems, and increased economic opportunities. Through training and knowledge-sharing, NATC seeks to empower farmers, promote gender equality, and encourage young people to build futures within their communities.

What We Do

NATC provides training in ecological and sustainable farming practices, including:

  • Crop production and demonstration plots
  • Agroforestry and integration of trees with farmland
  • Soil and water conservation techniques
  • Livestock and crop integration
  • Shade crop cultivation and forage systems
  • Pest management and climate resilience strategies

Impact in the Community

NATC has introduced improved agricultural practices that have significantly increased productivity and sustainability. One key example is the introduction of contour farming, where crops are planted across slopes rather than along them. This method reduces soil erosion, preserves fertility, and protects lowland rice fields from sediment damage.

Farmers are also trained in diversification and strategic planting techniques to reduce crop losses during periods of low rainfall. These methods help improve resilience and ensure more stable yields throughout the year.

Who We Serve

We serve local farmers, youth, and community members in Njawara and surrounding areas. Our programs focus on building practical skills, improving farm profitability, and promoting sustainable natural resource management.
